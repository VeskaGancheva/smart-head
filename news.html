News & project progress

Updates from the consortium — from online coordination to student engagement and in-person co-creation.

Autumn 2025

Online consortium meetings

Building the foundations for shared planning, methodology, research and delivery. The consortium began its work through regular online meetings on project planning, student needs, educational methodology, pilot resources and the future digital ecosystem.

27 November 2025

Students Workshop — TU Sofia

The first SMART-HEAD student workshop introduced the project objectives, partnership, planned digital ecosystem and the 17 UN SDGs. Through an interactive Mentimeter activity, students reflected on what a sustainable lifestyle means to them.

24–25 June 2026

Consortium Meeting — Cluj-Napoca

The four partner teams met at the Technical University of Cluj-Napoca for a hands-on workshop on creating digital educational resources: progress review, user research synthesis and work on the daily-card concept.

Student voices shape the learning design

December 2025 – April 2026: the consortium combined student research with the development and validation of pilot educational resources. The student needs survey and live card-design validation provided evidence-based guidance for the structure, visual language and interaction model of the future SMART-HEAD learning experience.
